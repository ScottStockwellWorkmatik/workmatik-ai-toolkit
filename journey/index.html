How to use this map.
  1. Pick an activity in either flow. Red shows what it needs first, green shows what it unlocks. Leader activities are blue, marketer activities are orange.
  2. Read the panel at the foot of the screen for the full activity, what “done” looks like and the linked activities. Select a linked activity to follow the chain.
  3. Bold IDs in a box’s “Needs first” line belong to the other flow, so they mark handovers between leader and marketer.

      About this map

      The 35 activities come from Workmatik’s AI readiness framework for marketing. Each appears once: 18 in the leader flow and 17 in the marketer flow. The five phases are Direct, Organise, Build, Scale and Sustain.

      The ID letter shows the category. A thick outline and a star mark the four activities proposed as critical.

      The allocation of activities to the two flows, the phases and the dependencies are the author’s judgement and have no published source. Treat them as a starting point to challenge and adapt for your team.
